Overview

Inventory keeps how much of each product is where, for each company separately, and what identifies it (lot numbers with expiry dates, machines' serial numbers). Every change to a quantity is a stock movement with a reason, kept in Movement History.

LocationWhat it isExample
WarehouseA store set up in Organization Profile → WarehousesMain Warehouse
Field stockWhat one of the company's own people (a specialist) carriesField stock — Ziyad
Consigned stockAnother company's stock placed with you (or yours placed elsewhere) — handled in the Consignment moduleConsigned → Affirma · Zilarahim
Each company has its own stock. Field stock is always with the company's own people; stock for another company goes by consignment or a sale.
02

Before You Start — a Named Warehouse

Inventory opens only when the company has a warehouse with a name. Until then every inventory page shows “Set up your warehouse first” and the Inventory menu shows ⚠ Set up warehouse with the other items greyed out.

1

Click ⚠ Set up warehouse

It opens Organization → Organization Profile at Warehouse addresses (highlighted, with a blank row ready).

2

Give it a name and address, then Save

e.g. Main Warehouse. A warehouse without a name can't be saved. The menu unlocks straight away.

Don't rename a warehouse that holds stock — existing stock records keep the old name. Ask an administrator if a rename is really needed.

06

New Movement

1

Location

Pick a warehouse or a specialist (your own people only). Nothing is preselected; the hint shows the address or whose field stock it is.

2

Product, type, quantity

The hint under Movement Type explains the chosen type. For machines, enter one serial number per unit coming in, or pick the units going out.

3

Lot, expiry, notes — Save

Write why in Notes. It is Pending until approved — or applied at once if you can approve.

Opening Balance+Starting quantity when you begin using the systemLater corrections
Stock In+Goods without a PO (supplier samples, stock found). Warehouses onlyPO goods — they come in through Goods Receipt
Stock Out−Damaged, expired, lost, internal use, samples given — say whySelling (DO), a case (Case DO), moving stock (Transfer)

Return+Goods back that aren't on any document (e.g. back from repair)Customer returns (DO → Record Return), a specialist's stock (Return from Rep), consigned stock (Consignment → Return)

Automatic movements (DO delivery, Case usage, Loan out/return, Field transfer, Consigned in/used/returned/moved…) are created by those screens and appear in Movement History with their document number.

07

Transfers & Field Stock

  • Transfer to Rep — warehouse → specialist, or back (Return from Rep). Only the company's own people; machines are picked by serial number.
  • Transfer Between Warehouses (Movement History) — always needs approval; one decision covers the out and in lines.
  • Stock requests — a specialist requests stock; when an approver approves, the stock is transferred at once.
  • Field Stock — each specialist's holding, consigned items tagged with their owner and left out of the stock value; their Movement History has a running balance that ends at what they hold.
08

Movement History

Every movement with type, quantity, balance after, reference (PO, DO, consignment…), who and when. A pending movement can still be edited (an Adjustment keeps its + / − direction); approved ones only their notes and reference. Stock that sister companies consigned to you is listed too, read-only, tagged with the owner.

09

Lots & Serial Numbers

What identifies the stock you hold. Quantities and Movement History never change here. Changing anything needs Manage Inventory.

Lots & expiry tab

Choose a warehouse or specialist. Tiles: Items · Without a lot · Expiring / expired (tap to filter). Each item shows a bar of how much is lotted, its lots with expiry status (OK / 45d left / expired), and:

  1. + Assign lot no. & expiry — quantity pre-filled with what has no lot (never more); split across lots by saving one at a time.
  2. Edit (pencil) — correct a lot number or expiry; its movements are updated too.

Serial numbers tab

Every machine: serial no., product, use (For sale / Company asset), status, where it is.

  1. Register serial numbers — location → item held there → one serial no. + use per machine still without one.
  2. Fix serial no. — a typo, with a reason (noted on the unit; its history follows).
  3. Remove — a serial entered by mistake, with a reason; not once used on a Case DO or consignment.
  4. Change the use; History; Mark returned (on loan).
Lots should be recorded when stock arrives (Goods Receipt, New Movement). This page is for stock that came in without one, and for corrections. Rental/loan/demo is chosen each time a company asset goes out — not stored on the unit.
10

Item Groups

Your own headings (e.g. milh, evlt, silac, filac). A product can be in several groups but is listed once — under the group matching the case type on a Case DO, else its first group. Stock Overview, Field Stock and the Case DO picker follow your order; “Other” is last.

11

Stock Rules

How strictly a Case DO must be covered by stock someone actually holds — per company. Use Record & flag while stock isn't reconciled (pilot), then switch to Enforce.

ModeUsage beyond what's heldWhen
Record & flagSaved; listed under Shortfalls to reconcilePilot (default)
WarnSaved after an amber warning, with a reasonLast weeks before going live
EnforceRefused (red) — fix the records first. People with Record Case DO usage without enough stock may override with a reason (flagged)Stock reconciled; can start on a set date (Warn until then)
  • When: checked when recording the actual items (on by default); optionally also when creating the Case DO.
  • Where stock may come from: the specialist's own field stock and consigned stock whose owner set terms; optionally Taken from a warehouse or another of your people; optionally negative balances (not in Enforce).
  • Exceptions: item groups or products still being counted — only flagged, never refused. Machines must always be held.
  • Ready to enforce? — open shortfalls, specialists not counted in 90 days, negative balances. A count = an approved Adjustment or Opening Balance.
  • Changes to these rules — who changed what, when.
Shortfalls to reconcile — each shows the DO, item, location, used / held / short, the reason. Fix the records with Transfer to … (the stock was really there) or Correct the count, then Mark resolved (transferred / corrected / explained). Cancelling the DO clears its open shortfalls.
12

Case DOs & Stock

  • Stock moves when the actual items are recorded (after the case), from the application specialist's field stock — consigned stock first, then their own (Affirma's setting).
  • Under Other items used, a catalogue item can be Taken from a warehouse or another specialist; it is deducted there.
  • Only what was really held is deducted; anything beyond is a shortfall (Stock Rules). Cancelling a Case DO puts back exactly what it took.
  • Consigned stock can be used only when its owner has set consignment terms for your company.

FAQ

Inventory says “Set up your warehouse first”

The company has no warehouse with a name. Add one in Organization Profile → Warehouses.

My movement didn't change the stock

It is pending — an approver approves it in Pending Approvals.

I counted less than the system shows

Adjust quantity → Adjustment ↕ → Decrease, with the count date in Notes.

Why can't I pick another company's person?

Field stock is only with your own people. Use consignment or a sale for another company.

Where do I edit a lot number or serial number?

Lots & Serial Numbers — Stock Overview only shows them.

A Case DO was refused for stock

Stock Rules are on Enforce. Transfer the stock to the specialist, take it from another location, or have the count corrected, then record again.
