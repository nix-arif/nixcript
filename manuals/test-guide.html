Before you start

  • Test on the dev system only. It is a fresh copy of production (02 Oct 2026) — real customers and stock, nothing you do touches production.
  • Switch company with the workspace switcher (top-left). Stock owner = Smart Innosys; sister company / agent = Affirma (specialist Zilarahim).
  • No machines are registered yet — UC-02 sets one up. Do the use cases in order; later ones use what earlier ones create.
  • Products have no unit cost in this data, so anything priced “cost-plus” shows pending — use the price lists as described.
  • Good test items: SFCS000345, SSP-8030 (have MDA certificates) and A.4086.1, STRC1406C (no MDA).
  • Something not as expected? Note the use-case number, step and a screenshot.

UC-02

Register machines with serial numbers

Smart Innosys · owner / logistic
  1. Product → find a machine product (e.g. SWING 15) and mark it Rental-capable (machine / equipment).
  2. Inventory → Movement History → New Movement: Warehouse Main Warehouse, product SWING 15, type Opening Balance.
  3. Tick Each unit has its own serial number (already ticked for machines). Quantity 3.
  4. Enter only 2 serial numbers and Save.
  5. Enter the 3rd serial number; set two to Company asset and one to For sale. Save.
  6. Try New Movement again with one of the same serial numbers.

UC-19

Case DO in two steps: customer items now, actual items after the case

Affirma · DO user
The doctor's template is the customer copy: what the hospital sees (with MDA certificates) and is billed for. What the specialist actually used is recorded after the case; only then is stock deducted and the internal copy available.
  1. Fulfillment → Case Templates → Mr Atiki → edit or new. Selling price Itemized. Items: SFCS000345 × 1 at RM 500; type MILH procedure kit and choose Use “MILH procedure kit” as a line without a product, RM 300; SWING 15 × 1 → Machine to pick Company asset (lent) → Loan → Stays at hospital → fee 250. Save.
  2. New Case DO → specialist Zilarahim → doctor Mr Atiki (the template fills Customer copy items and the price). Leave The case is done — record now unticked → Create Case DO.
  3. Open the DO → download the Customer copy (with price). Try the Internal copy.
  4. Click Record actual items → check the prefilled items (SWING 15 serial ticked, fee 250) → Record actual items.
  5. Download both copies again; open Create Invoice from the DO (don't save). Then click undo recording and record again.

UC-20

Your own item groups

Inventory manager · Case DO user
Groups are yours to define (any names, e.g. matching your case types: milh, evlt, pldd). A product can be in several groups, but every stock list shows it once — under its first group, or the group that matches the case type on a Case DO — and names its other groups on the row. Groups never change stock.
  1. Inventory → Item Groups. Add your groups (any name, any colour) and use ↑ ↓ to put them in the order you want.
  2. Under Products you hold, click a group chip on a product to put it in (+ evlt → ✓ evlt) or take it out. Give SWING 15 ✓ milh ✓ evlt ✓ pldd. (Or: a group's Add products search, or tick several products and Add selected to….)
  3. Open Inventory → Stock Overview, then Field Stock → a specialist.
  4. New Case DO → choose a specialist → tick case type milh, then untick it and tick evlt. Do not save.
