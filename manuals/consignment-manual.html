01

Overview

Consignment lets a company place its stock with someone else while keeping ownership until the stock is used. Only what is used is billed. Anything unused can come back.

Everything lives under the Consignment menu: Consignments, New, External agents, Balance, Settlement and Settings.

The company that owns the stock is the owner. It can place stock with three kinds of consignee:

Golden rule: consigned stock stays on the owner's books, at a consignment location, until it is used. Only then does ownership pass and a bill follow.

03

Key Concepts

A consignment is one shipment document, numbered like CSSI/26-0001, from one owner warehouse to one consignment location. Each product line tracks what is still out:

On hand = Sent − Consumed − Returned − Adjusted

Consignment locations

Where consigned stock sits. It still appears in the owner's inventory.

LocationUsed for
Agent company warehouseStock placed with your other company, not yet with a person
Agent specialistStock carried by one application specialist of your other company — their Case DOs draw from it
External agentStock held by a dealer or sales agent
Customer siteStock kept at a hospital

Events

Every change is recorded as an event on the consignment. Events drive both the balance and the bill.

EventWhat happenedBilled?
SendStock left the owner warehouse for the locationNo
ConsumeStock was used — by a Case DO or a usage reportYes
ReturnUnused stock came back to the owner warehouseNo
AdjustA count found stock lost, damaged, expired or shortOnly if "charge the consignee" is ticked
ReverseA Case DO that used consigned stock was deleted, so the use is undoneCancels the consume

A billable event stays unsettled until a settlement picks it up. Once settled it is locked and can no longer be reversed.

Lots and serials carry through. A lot-tracked line keeps its lot and expiry. A serial-tracked unit shows Consigned while it is out and Sold once used.

Setting Up

Set up once per company under Consignment → Settings, before the first consignment. Each company decides its own settings.

When this company is the agent

Which stock a Case DO uses first when a specialist carries both consigned stock and the company's own:

  1. Consigned stock first — owner's stock is used and settled sooner
  2. Own stock first — consigned stock only when own stock runs out

When this company is the owner

How often consignment locations should be counted:

  1. Monthly
  2. Quarterly
  3. Only when needed

One card per sister company (owner → agent)

The owner decides how stock placed with each of its other companies is handled and billed.

Transfer price — worked example

The transfer price is what the owner charges its sister company for each consigned unit the sister company uses. Example: Smart Innosys consigns a stent to Affirma. Smart Innosys bought it for RM 1,000. Affirma uses 1 unit on a Case DO and invoices the hospital RM 1,800.

MethodSettingCalculationSmart Innosys bills Affirma
Cost-plusMarkup 10%RM 1,000 cost + 10%RM 1,100
Fixed price listStent listed at RM 1,200The listed price, whatever the cost or saleRM 1,200
% of selling priceShare 60%RM 1,800 hospital price × 60%RM 1,080

Cost-plus

The owner's cost plus a fixed markup. Cost is the unit cost recorded when the stock was sent, or the product's cost price.

  1. Good for: simple, stable margin for the owner
  2. Needs: a unit cost on the product — without one the line stays pending
  3. Agent's margin moves with its own selling price

Fixed price list

An agreed intercompany price per product. A product not on the list falls back to cost-plus with the markup you set (e.g. a gauze with cost RM 50 and 10% markup → RM 55).

  1. Good for: agreed prices that don't change with cost
  2. Needs: the list kept up to date
  3. Both sides know the price in advance

% of selling price

A share of what the agent actually charged. If Affirma sells the same stent for RM 2,000 next time, Smart Innosys gets RM 1,200 (60%).

  1. Good for: sharing the upside and the risk of the sale
  2. Needs: Affirma's invoice for the Case DO — until it exists the line shows Waiting for the Case DO's invoice
  3. Only works for use through Case DOs

Which to choose?

Owner wants a guaranteed margin → Cost-plus. Both companies agreed prices up front → Fixed price list. Owner shares in what the agent earns → % of selling price. You can change the method any time; it applies to lines settled afterwards.

With Fixed price list, a price list editor appears under the card: search a product, add it, type the price and click away to save. The bin icon removes it.

If a price can't be worked out — for example cost-plus on a product with no unit cost — the line stays pending instead of being billed at RM 0. Fix the product cost or add a price and it settles next time.

An external agent is an outside party holding your stock. Add them under Consignment → External agents → New agent and choose how each one works.

Dealer price — pick one

  • Discount off selling price — product selling price less a discount %
  • Dealer price list — a price per product, edited on the agent's form after the first save; unlisted products use the discount
  • Cost-plus — your unit cost plus a markup %

Every agent also has contact details, settlement Manual / Automatic, frequency Monthly / Per report, and an Active switch. An inactive agent keeps its stock and history but can't receive new consignments.

The first settlement creates a linked customer record (for dealer invoices) or supplier record (for commission POs) automatically.

You can't switch an agent between dealer and sales agent while it has unbilled usage. Settle first, then switch.

Sending Stock

Consignments are always created from the owner company. Switch the workspace to the owner (e.g. Smart Innosys) first.

1

Open Consignment → New

Or click Create Consignment on a sales order — the hospital is preselected and the consignment is linked to that SO.

2

Choose who to send to

Your other company: pick the company, then its warehouse or a named application specialist (choose the specialist if they will use it on Case DOs). External agent: pick the dealer or sales agent. Customer (hospital): search and pick the hospital.

3

Pick the warehouse and date

Choose From warehouse (where the stock leaves) and the Sent date. Changing the warehouse clears the item list.

4

Add items

Search stock in that warehouse. Plain products: enter the quantity. Lot-tracked: choose the lot, then the quantity. Serial-tracked: tick each serial number.

5

Send consignment

Add notes if needed and click Send consignment. Stock moves to the consignment location and you land on the new consignment's page.

Nothing moves if any line is wrong. The message lists every problem — e.g. more than the warehouse holds, a lot-tracked product without a lot, or a serial unit that isn't in stock.
08

Managing a Consignment

Open a consignment from Consignment → Consignments (filter by All, Company, External or Customer). Its page shows every line with Sent, Consumed, Returned, Adjusted and On hand, plus the full event history. The owner has four actions:

Record usage

For hospitals and external agents. Enter what was used from their usage sheet.

  1. Set the date and a reference (usage sheet or hospital PO no.)
  2. External agent: pick the hospital where it was used (required for sales agents)
  3. Enter the quantity per line (serials: tick the unit)
  4. Sales agent: enter the price charged per line (blank = catalogue price)
  5. Click Confirm usage

Return to warehouse

Unused stock comes back to the warehouse it was sent from. Enter the quantity per line and confirm. Serial units return to In stock.

Adjust after count

After a physical count, write off what's missing. Choose a reason — Lost, Damaged, Expired or Count difference — and whether to charge the consignee. Charged adjustments are billed at settlement. Needs the Count Adjustments permission.

Close consignment

Once nothing is on hand, close it. A consignment with stock still out can't be closed — return, record usage or adjust first.

Usage is taken from the exact line you enter it on, so the right consignment and lot are used. With per-report settlement set, the invoice is created as soon as you confirm.

Case DOs (Agent Company)

When stock is consigned to a specialist of your other company, that company uses it through its normal Case DOs — nobody records usage by hand. Consigned stock is used when the Case DO's actual items are recorded (after the case, or at creation if the case is already done) — see the Case DO Manual.

  • The specialist's field stock shows consigned items with a Consigned tag
  • When the actual items are recorded, consigned and own stock are used in the order set in the agent company's settings (consigned first by default); any remainder comes from own stock
  • The oldest consignment is used first, earliest expiry first
  • Each use becomes a billable consume event, linked to the DO number
  • With per use automatic settlement, the intercompany invoice and PO are created as soon as the Case DO's actual items are recorded
Deleting a Case DO, or undoing its recorded actual items, reverses its consigned use and puts the stock back at the specialist. If that use was already settled, it is refused — cancel or credit the settlement documents first.

Moving consigned stock inside the agent company

The agent company can move stock consigned to it between its own warehouse and its specialists without asking the owner. Ownership doesn't change: it is still the owner's stock and still with the agent, only its location changes, and the owner sees the new location. This is the industry practice for consignment stock: internal moves are the consignee's job; placing the stock with anyone outside the company is the owner's decision.

MoveWho
Warehouse → specialist, specialist → warehouse, specialist → specialistThe agent company, by users who can create stock transfers. No owner approval needed.
To the agent's customers (hospitals) or another companyThe owner only. The Allow pass-on setting covers this; that flow is not built yet.
Use on a case, return to the owner, count adjustmentUnchanged: Case DO (agent), or return and adjust on the consignment (owner).
1

Open Inventory → Stock Overview

Switch to the agent company. Consigned stock is under Consigned in — owned by others, grouped by location (Warehouse, or With <specialist>) and owner. The same list is on Consignment → Balance under Held for others.

2

Click Move on the location the stock is at

Choose where it goes: Warehouse or Specialist — name.

3

Pick the items

Enter a quantity per lot, or tick each machine by serial number. You can't move more than is at that location. A machine that is out at a hospital can't be moved until it is back.

4

Click Move

The stock is now at the new location. A specialist sees it in Field Stock with the Consigned tag, and their Case DOs use it as usual.

What is recorded:

  • The quantity leaves the original consignment (its Moved column) and goes onto the owner's open consignment for the new location. One is created automatically if needed, e.g. CSSI/26-0002 — Moved within Affirma from CSSI/26-0001.
  • Both consignments show the move in their history (Moved out / Moved in), linked to each other.
  • Movement History shows it for both companies: the agent sees Consigned moved, the owner sees Consignment Moved.
  • Moving doesn't bill anything. Monthly machine rental follows the machine without charging the same month twice.

Machines (Company Assets)

A machine (e.g. a phaco machine or drill console) is lent for a case and comes back. It is never used up or sold through consignment. Consumables on the same case are used up as usual.

Every serial-numbered unit is one of two things — decided once, when it is registered:

CategoryMeaning
For saleStock to sell — used up / sold on a case, like any item.
Company assetStays the organisation's (machine / equipment). Never sold through a case — lent out and comes back.

Why a company asset is out is chosen each time it goes out, not fixed on the machine — the same machine can be a demo this month and on rental next month:

PurposeTypical useUsage fee
RentalHired out for the caseTicked by default — enter the fee
LoanLent, e.g. while the hospital's own machine is repairedNot charged by default
DemoDemonstration / trialNot charged by default
Register machines in Inventory → Movement History → New Movement: tick Each unit has its own serial number, enter the quantity, then one serial number per machine (the count must match the quantity) and choose For sale or Company asset. Correct it later under Inventory → Serialized Units or Stock Overview → Edit.

On a Case DO

When the actual items are recorded (after the case) and a company asset is ticked, these choices appear under it — prefilled from the doctor's template:

Purpose — Rental / Loan / Demo

Why the machine goes out on this case. Rental ticks Charge usage fee; Loan and Demo untick it (you can still change it). The purpose shows on the DO, on Field Stock and on both Case DO copies (e.g. “Machine on loan — returned after the case”).

Returned same day / Stays at hospital

Returned same day (default): the machine goes out and comes straight back with the specialist; the case still shows in its history. Stays at hospital: it shows At the hospital on the DO until someone clicks Return to specialist in the DO's Machines box.

Charge usage fee

Tick it and enter the fee per case if the hospital pays for using the machine this time. Leave it unticked when the machine is free (e.g. included with consumables). The fee fills in automatically when you Create Invoice from the Case DO.

A Case DO can be invoiced as soon as it is saved — it doesn't need to be marked delivered. The usage fee is added once the actual items are recorded.

Selling a company asset

Where the machine isHow to sell it
At the hospital on a Case DOOn the DO's Machines box click Sell to hospital, enter the price, Confirm sale. It becomes For sale → Sold, never comes back, and the sale is added as its own line when you Create Invoice from the DO (if the DO was already invoiced, invoice the sale separately). A consigned machine is also billed by its owner at the transfer price.

Machines owned by another company or placed with an agent

The owner decides, per sister company and per external agent, how its machines are charged. Set it in Consignment → Settings (sister company card) or External agents (agent form), under Machines (rental units).

OptionWho can use itWhat the owner bills
No chargeSister company, dealer, sales agentNothing — machine use is only recorded
Fixed fee per caseSister company, dealerA set amount every time the machine is used on a case
Share of the usage feeSister company, dealerA % of the usage fee they charged the hospital. No fee charged → nothing
Monthly rentalSister company, dealerA set amount per machine for every month they hold it, however often it's used
Invoice the hospital per caseSales agentThe hospital is invoiced the usage fee entered with each case; commission on it is optional

Worked example

Smart Innosys places a phaco machine with Affirma. Affirma uses it on a case and charges the hospital a RM 500 usage fee.

SettingSmart Innosys bills Affirma
No chargeRM 0
Fixed fee per case, RM 100RM 100 for this case
Share of the usage fee, 40%RM 200 (40% of RM 500)
Monthly rental, RM 300RM 300 for the month, no matter how many cases
  • Sister company: machine use is recorded automatically from Affirma's Case DOs.
  • Dealer, sales agent or hospital: open the consignment, click Record usage, tick used on a case on the machine, choose its purpose (Rental / Loan / Demo) and enter the usage fee (blank = no fee). Sales agents also pick the hospital.
  • Machine kept at a hospital (customer consignment): the usage fee you enter is invoiced to that hospital.
  • The charge is fixed when the use is recorded — changing the setting later only affects later uses.
  • Monthly rental is added for each month the machine was held when you preview or generate a settlement (and by the automatic monthly run).
  • The consignment page shows each machine with a Machine tag and how many cases it was used on.
Deleting a Case DO, or undoing its recorded actual items, puts its machines back with the specialist. If a consigned machine's use on it was already settled, the delete is refused.

Settlement

Consignment → Settlement bills what has been used. Only billable events are included. Needs the Settle permission.

TabHow to settleDocuments created
Our companiesPick the agent company and a month (or tick all unsettled) → Preview → Generate invoice + PODraft invoice from the owner to the agent + confirmed PO in the agent company, linked to each other
External agents — dealerPick the dealer and month → Preview → Generate invoiceDraft invoice to the dealer at the dealer price
External agents — sales agentPick the agent and month → Preview (shows hospital and commission per line) → Generate invoices + commission POOne draft invoice per hospital + a confirmed commission PO to the agent (service, no goods receipt)

Lines that can't be priced yet show pending with the reason (e.g. no selling price, no unit cost). They're left for later and don't block the rest. A sales-agent line without a hospital is also pending.

Automatic settlement

  • Per use / per report — runs immediately after each Case DO or usage report
  • Monthly — runs once a month for the previous month, for every company pair and active external agent set to Automatic + Monthly
  • Settled events are stamped, so nothing is billed twice

The Settlement history at the bottom lists every settlement with its invoices, POs and commission.

Balance & History

  • Consignment → Balance — all your stock still out, grouped by location (sister company, specialist, external agent, hospital), with lot and serial
  • Consignment detail — the full event history of one consignment, including hospital and price for usage
  • Inventory → Field Stock — the specialist's view. Consigned items are tagged with the owner and left out of the stock value. The Movement History tab lists every movement of the specialist's own and consigned stock (received, used, returned, moved), with the quantity and a running Balance from the specialist's side. The balances end at what Current Holdings shows.
  • External agents — each agent's card shows how many of your units it holds
  • Receiving company (agent) — Inventory → Stock Overview lists what a sister company consigned to you under Consigned in — owned by others (filter button Consigned in), and Consignment → Balance shows it under Held for others. Quantity, lot and serial only, with no value. Inventory → Movement History lists every movement of it (received, used, returned to owner, count adjustments), tagged with the owner, with quantities and balances from your side. Filter button: Consigned in. These rows are read-only.
Why consigned-in stock is not in your own stock: this is the accounting standard (MFRS 15 / IFRS 15). The sender still owns and controls the goods until you use or sell them, so they stay in the sender's stock and stock value. You see them so your shelf count matches and you know what you can use. Use, return or adjust them through the consignment, not with stock movements.

FAQ

Why is a line "pending" in settlement?

Its price can't be worked out — usually the product has no unit cost or selling price, or a sales-agent use has no hospital. Fix the product or price list and preview again.

Can the agent company send or return stock?

It can't return stock to the owner, adjust it or settle it; the owner does that. It can move consigned stock between its own warehouse and specialists (Stock Overview → Consigned in → Move).

Why isn't consigned-in stock in my stock value?

It is still the owner's stock (MFRS 15 / IFRS 15) until you use it. You see it, by quantity only, under Consigned in in Stock Overview and Movement History.

Why can't I delete this Case DO?

It used consigned stock that is already settled. Deal with the settlement invoice/PO first.

Why can't I close the consignment?

Stock is still on hand. Return it, record its usage, or adjust it after a count.
