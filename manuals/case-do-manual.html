01

Overview

A Case DO records one case (procedure) for a doctor: what the hospital receives and pays for, and what the application specialist actually used from their field stock.

It keeps these as two lists, because they can differ:

ListWhat it isWhere it goesWhen
Customer copy itemsWhat the hospital sees and is billed for — from the doctor's templateCustomer copy (with MDA certificates) and the invoiceWhen the DO is created; editable until invoiced
Actual items usedWhat the specialist really used from field stock, including which machine (serial no.)Internal copy; stock and consignmentAfter the case — stock is deducted only then

Everything is under Fulfillment → Delivery Orders (Case DOs) and Fulfillment → Case Templates.

02

The Two Steps

1

Before or at the case — create the Case DO

Pick the specialist and the doctor. The doctor's template fills the customer copy items and the selling price. No stock moves. The draft has a temporary reference (e.g. DRAFT-FPQXLSAQ), not a DO number; its PDF is stamped DRAFT.

2

After the case — record the actual items

On the DO page click Record actual items, tick what was really used (and the machine's serial no.). Stock is deducted now, the internal copy becomes available, and the customer copy shows the machine's serial no.

3

Mark as delivered — the DO number

Click Mark as Delivered. The DO now gets its DO number (e.g. DOAF/26-0311), and every stock and consignment record made under the temporary reference is updated to it. Print the customer copy for the hospital now.

4

Invoice

Create Invoice from the delivered DO — it starts with the customer items and their prices (plus machine usage fees).

Why a draft has no number: numbers are given only on delivery, so deleting or cancelling a draft never leaves a gap in the DO numbering.
If the DO is created after the case is already done, tick The case is done — record the items actually used now on the create form to do both steps at once.

Until step 2 is done, the Delivery Orders list shows the DO with the tag actual items to record.

04

Doctor Templates

A template is the customer copy DO. Everything in it is what the hospital receives on the customer copy and is invoiced for. What the specialist actually uses is recorded on the DO after the case (internal copy).

A template is a doctor's usual case: case category, description, the customer copy items and their selling price. Each company keeps its own templates — a template made in Smart Innosys is used only on Smart Innosys Case DOs (doctors and products can still be any of the group's). Open Fulfillment → Case Templates → New template (or the pencil on an existing one).

1

Doctor, hospital, name, category, description

A doctor who works at several hospitals can keep a template per hospital — choose the hospital, or Any of the doctor's hospitals. The description prints on the DO (e.g. “Laser haemorrhoidoplasty (MILH), grade III”).

2

Customer copy items

Search a product — it prints with its MDA number and certificate. A product without a valid MDA registration is shown in red (in the template and on the Case DO form): it will not print on the customer copy — update the product's MDA details or remove it. Or type a name and choose Use “…” as a line without a product for a line that prints as written, e.g. MILH procedure kit.

3

Selling price

Itemized or Total — see below.

4

Save

A doctor's first template for a hospital is their default there: it fills in automatically on a Case DO for that doctor at that hospital. Use make default to change it. Each hospital (and “any hospital”) has its own default.

A Case DO can also be saved as a template: Save this case as a template on the create form.

Selling price

Itemized

Each item has its own price (filled from the product's selling price, changeable). The invoice lists each item at its price.

Total

One price for the whole case. The invoice has one Case package line at that price; the items are listed as “included”.

A machine lent on the case (company asset) is charged its usage fee (e.g. rental RM 2,000 per case): with Itemized pricing the fee is the machine's price on the customer copy and invoice (shown in its price column); with Total it is charged on top of the case price.

Itemized price and items without MDA don't mix. A product without a valid MDA registration is left off the customer copy, so with a price per item the customer copy total wouldn't match what is invoiced. A red warning appears, and saving the template, creating the Case DO or saving the DO's customer items is refused until you remove the item, update its MDA details, or switch to a Total price.

Machines in a template

For a machine (e.g. SWING 15) choose Machine to pick:

  • Company asset (lent) — with purpose (Rental / Loan / Demo), Returned same day or Stays at hospital, and the usage fee
  • For sale (sold) — the machine is sold to the hospital
  • Any — choose the unit when recording

When the actual items are recorded, a unit of that kind is ticked from the specialist's holding with these choices filled in.

05

Step 1 — Create the Case DO

1

Delivery Orders → New → Case DO

Case date, MRN no., case category.

2

Application specialist

Whose field stock the items come from (the sales person is the same person unless you untick it). Only your company's own people are listed. If Stock Rules check on creation, the customer items are checked against what they hold now (see Not enough stock).

3

Customer (doctor) and hospital

Once the hospital is known (chosen, or the doctor's only one), that hospital's default template — else the “any hospital” default — fills in everything: case category, description, Customer copy items, Selling price and the machine choices. Only templates for that hospital (and “any hospital”) show as buttons. Switching hospital switches to its template.

4

Check the customer copy items and price

Add, change or remove lines for this case. These are what the hospital sees and pays for.

5

Create Case DO

Leave The case is done — record now unticked unless the case is already over. Nothing is taken from stock yet.

06

Step 2 — Record Actual Items

After the case, open the DO and click Record actual items (the amber box Actual items used — not recorded yet).

1

Check the prefilled items

The specialist's field stock is listed (under your item groups); the customer copy items they hold are ticked with their quantities. Free-text lines (e.g. a kit name) are not stock and are skipped.

2

Adjust to what was really used

Change quantities, tick or untick items, pick the lot if asked. For machines, tick the unit (serial no.) used and check its purpose, return mode and usage fee. Other items used is for anything not from the specialist's field stock — for a catalogue item, choose Taken from (a warehouse or another of your people) and it is deducted there.

3

Record actual items

Stock is checked against the Stock Rules, then taken out of the specialist's field stock — consigned stock first or last as set in Consignment Settings — and machines are lent or sold. The specialist sees “Your Case DOs: record the items used” on their dashboard until this is done.

Recorded the wrong items? On the DO page click undo recording: all stock (machines and consigned stock included) goes back to the specialist and you can record again. Undo is refused if consigned stock it used is already settled.

Item groups in the picker

The specialist's stock is listed under your item groups (Inventory → Item Groups). When the case category matches a group name — e.g. category milh and group milh — that group is first, open and marked matches case type; other groups are folded. A product in several groups (e.g. SWING 15 in milh, evlt and pldd) appears once, under the group matching the case. After recording, the DO page shows the picked items under the same groups.

Not enough stock (Stock Rules)

If the items recorded are more than the specialist (or the Taken from location) holds, a coloured box opens before anything is saved. What it allows depends on Inventory → Stock Rules:

ModeBoxYou can
Record & flag— (no box)Saved; the difference is listed as a shortfall for the inventory team
WarnAmber — “Not enough stock”Record anyway with a reason, or go back and fix
EnforceRed — “Not enough stock — can't record this” (or “… override needed” if you may override)Go back: transfer the stock to the specialist, use Taken from, or ask for the count to be corrected. With the override permission: Override and record with a reason

The box lists each item: needed, held, short. Only what is really held is deducted — the rest is a shortfall (Inventory → Stock Rules → Shortfalls) to reconcile; cancelling the DO clears it. Items in an exception group are only flagged. See the Inventory Manual.

07

The DO Page

  • Customer copy items — with the selling price. Click edit to change items, quantities, prices or the price mode — allowed until the DO is invoiced, then fixed.
  • Actual items used — the amber not recorded yet box with Record actual items, or the recorded list with the time and undo recording.
  • Machines — machines lent on this case: Return to specialist or Sell to hospital (see section 11).
  • Customer copy / Internal copy buttons, and with price / no price for the PDF.
  • A warning lists customer items that won't print because they have no valid MDA registration.

09

Invoice

On the DO page click Create Invoice. It is prefilled from the customer copy items:

  • Itemized — each customer item at its price
  • Total — one Case package — … line at the case price; the items as “(included)” at 0
  • Machine lent — its usage fee on the machine's line (once the actual items are recorded)
  • Machine sold to the hospital — its sale as its own line

A Case DO is invoiced once it is marked delivered (which needs its actual items recorded) — a draft has no DO number for the invoice to refer to. Once invoiced, its customer copy items can no longer be edited.

The invoice number follows the DO: DOAF/26-0311 is invoiced as INVAF/26-0311. A DO has one live invoice; to re-issue, cancel the invoice and create it again — it becomes INVAF/26-0311.2. Invoices without a DO (e.g. consignment settlements) use their own series, INVAF/26-C0001, so they never take a DO's number.
10

Cancelling a Case DO

A Case DO that has been issued is cancelled, not deleted: it stays in the list marked Cancelled, with who, when and why — so the DO numbers and the movement history still explain themselves.

Cancel, not “Mark as Returned”. A Case DO has no Mark as Returned: items used on a patient can't come back, and a machine comes back through Return to specialist in the Machines box. If the DO itself was wrong, cancel it.
1

Open the DO → Cancel DO

Needs the Cancel Delivery Order permission.

2

Give the reason → Cancel DO

e.g. “Case postponed by the doctor”.

What happens
StockEverything the DO took — own stock, consigned stock, machines, items Taken from elsewhere — goes back where it came from; open shortfalls are cleared
Movement HistoryKept; reversing entries are added (“DO cancelled — stock returned”) next to the original ones
The DOStatus Cancelled with the reason, date and person; locked — no recording, editing, invoicing or machine actions; PDFs stamped CANCELLED
Refused whenAn invoice is linked (cancel or credit the invoice first), or consigned stock it used is already settled
Delete vs Cancel vs Undo. Delete is only for a draft made by mistake — before any actual items are recorded. Undo recording corrects the actual items but keeps the DO. Cancel voids the whole case.
11

Machines After the Case

Returned same day

Lent and back with the specialist in one go; the case shows in the machine's history.

Stays at hospital

Shows At the hospital in the DO's Machines box and on Field Stock until someone clicks Return to specialist.

Hospital buys it

In the Machines box click Sell to hospital, enter the price — it is sold and added to the invoice.

A machine tagged For sale is sold to the hospital when ticked, not lent. Consigned machines (owned by a sister company) are billed by their owner as set in Consignment Settings — see the Consignment Manual.

Worked Example

Mr Atiki · MILH · specialist Zilarahim. Template (itemized): SFCS000345 × 1 at RM 500 · “MILH procedure kit” × 1 at RM 300 · SWING 15 company asset, Loan, stays at hospital, fee RM 250.

1

Create

Customer copy: SFCS000345 (MDA) RM 500 · MILH procedure kit RM 300 · SWING 15 (MDA). Stock unchanged; DO tagged actual items to record.

2

Record after the case

Prefilled: SFCS000345 × 1 and SWING 15 unit LD15421JW (Loan, stays, RM 250). Record → 1 SFCS000345 leaves Zilarahim's stock (consigned first), LD15421JW goes on loan.

3

Copies

Customer copy now shows SWING 15 “Serial No: LD15421JW · on loan — left at the hospital” and RM 250 — total RM 1,050. Internal copy: SFCS000345 × 1, SWING 15 LD15421JW.

4

Deliver

Mark as Delivered → DRAFT-… becomes DOAF/26-0311 on the DO, its stock movements and the consignment uses.

5

Invoice

SFCS000345 RM 500 · MILH procedure kit RM 300 · SWING 15 usage fee RM 250 = RM 1,050.

13

FAQ

Why can't I download the internal copy?

Either the actual items aren't recorded yet, or you don't have Download Case DO Internal Copy — ask an owner to add it to your role.

An item is missing from the customer copy

It has no valid MDA registration on the case date. Update the product's MDA details, or use a free-text line if that is acceptable.

The hospital wants a different item name

Edit the customer copy item's description (or the template). The actual item recorded is unaffected.

I can't edit the customer items

The DO is already invoiced — change the invoice instead.

My DO shows DRAFT-… instead of a number

A draft has a temporary reference. The DO number is given when you click Mark as Delivered (after the actual items are recorded).

Mark as Delivered is greyed out

Record the actual items used first — a Case DO is delivered once the case is done.

Stock didn't go down after creating the DO

That's expected: stock moves when the actual items are recorded (step 2).

Why is there no Delete button?

The DO has already taken stock (or is invoiced). Cancel it instead — it needs the Cancel Delivery Order permission.

“Not enough stock” — but the specialist has it

The system's record is behind. Transfer the stock to the specialist (Inventory → Transfer to Rep) or have their count corrected, then record again. On Record & flag or Warn you can save now and reconcile later.

The specialist isn't in the list

Only your company's own members are listed. A person from a sister company uses stock through consignment.

How do I change the notes or bank on the DO?

Organization → Document Settings → Table → Delivery order — below the table. The bank is your primary account in Organization Profile → Banking.

What about Case DOs made before this change?

They keep working as before: their items were deducted when created, and their customer copy is made from those items.
