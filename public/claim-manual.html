01

Overview

The Claim module lets you submit and track expense reimbursement requests — covering local travel, overseas trips, and customer entertainment. Each claim moves through a review chain and is automatically posted as a journal entry once approved.

You can submit immediately or save a draft and finalize later. Depending on your organisation's setup, a Checker may review the claim before it reaches the Approver.

Every claim gets a unique application number: CR-YYYY-XXXX (e.g. CR-2026-0001). Quote this number when following up on a claim.

03

How to Submit a Claim

1

Navigate to My Claims

From the sidebar go to Human Resources → My Claims. This lists all your past and active claims.

2

Click "New Claim"

Use the button at the top-right of the My Claims page to open the submission form.

3

Select Claim Type

Choose Local, Overseas, or Entertainment Form. See Section 02 if you're unsure which applies.

4

Fill In Claim Details

Complete the header (claim period, purpose, department) and add each expense as a line item. Attach receipts where required. For travel, enter distance and route. For overseas FX, enter the foreign-currency amount — the system records the exchange rate.

5

Submit or Save as Draft

Choose how to proceed:

Submit Now

Claim is sent immediately. Status becomes Pending and reviewers are notified.

Save as Draft

Claim is saved privately as Draft. Click Finalize when ready to submit.

05

Roles & Responsibilities

Three roles participate in the claim process. A user may hold more than one role.

claim:submit

Submitter

Create new claims (Local, Overseas, Entertainment)
Save claims as drafts and finalize when ready
Cancel claims before approval
Resubmit rejected claims after editing
View own claim history

claim:check

Checker

Review claims in the Checker queue
Approve → Checked
Reject with reason → Rejected
View all claims checked in the organisation

claim:approve

Approver

Review Checked claims (or Pending if no Checker)
Approve → Approved then Posted
Reject with reason → Rejected
View all approved claim history
No Checker assigned? If no user holds the claim:check role, the Checker step is skipped entirely — claims go directly from Pending to Approver review.
06

Managing Your Claims

Resubmitting a Rejected Claim

If rejected, you receive a notification with the reviewer's reason. You can edit and resubmit from the claim detail page.

  1. Open the rejected claim from My Claims
  2. Read the rejection reason shown at the top
  3. Edit claim details or line items as needed
  4. Click Resubmit
  5. Status returns to Pending and reviewers are notified again

Cancelling a Claim

Cancellation is available when the claim is in Draft, Pending, or Checked status.

  1. Open the claim from My Claims
  2. Click Cancel Claim
  3. Confirm the cancellation
  4. Status changes to Cancelled — this cannot be undone

Claims that are Approved or Posted cannot be cancelled.

Line Item Categories

Each line item must be assigned a category. Choose the one that best describes the expense.

Local Claim

TRAVEL

Travel — Mileage

Own vehicle or car pool trips. Enter route, distance, and travel mode.

TRAVEL_DAILY_ALLOWANCE

Daily Allowance

Per-diem allowance associated with a travel trip.

TRAVEL_ACCOMMODATION

Accommodation

Hotel or lodging costs during a local business trip.

TRAVEL_ENTERTAINMENT

Travel Entertainment

Entertainment expenses incurred while travelling.

TOLL / PARKING

Toll & Parking

Highway toll charges and parking fees.

MOBILE

Mobile Phone

Business-related phone charges.

IN_BASE_ENT

In-Base Entertainment

Customer or client entertainment at the home base location.

OTHER_LOCAL

Other Local

Miscellaneous local expenses not covered by other categories.

Overseas Claim

OVERSEAS_MYR

Overseas — MYR

Overseas expenses paid in Malaysian Ringgit.

OVERSEAS_FX

Overseas — Foreign Currency

Expenses in foreign currency. Enter the FX amount; the system records the exchange rate.

OVERSEAS_OTHER

Overseas — Other

Other overseas expenses not covered above.
