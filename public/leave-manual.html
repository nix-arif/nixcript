01

Overview

The Leave module lets you apply for, track, and manage leave applications — from annual leave to medical and emergency leave. Applications are submitted directly for Approver review; there is no draft or checker step.

Leave balances are calculated per calendar year based on your length of service. The system counts working days only (Monday to Friday) and prevents overlapping applications.

Every application gets a unique number: LV-YYYY-XXXX (e.g. LV-2026-0001). Use this number when following up with HR.

03

Your Leave Balance

Go to Human Resources → My Leave to see your balances for the current year. Each leave type shows:

Entitled

16

Days granted this year based on service

Carry Forward

5

Unused days brought over from last year

Used

4

Approved and consumed days

Available

17

Remaining days you can apply for

Available = Entitled + Carry Forward − Used − Pending

Days in Pending status are reserved from your balance until the application is approved or cancelled. Weekends are not counted in any balance.

04

How to Apply for Leave

1

Check Your Balance

Go to Human Resources → My Leave and verify you have enough available days for the leave type you need.

2

Click "Apply Leave"

Use the button at the top-right of the My Leave page to open the application form.

3

Select Leave Type

Choose from the available leave types in the dropdown. Only active types with sufficient balance are selectable.

4

Set Dates

Pick your start and end date. The system automatically calculates the number of working days (Monday–Friday only). Weekends and public holidays are excluded.

For half-day leave, enable the toggle and select AM (morning) or PM (afternoon). See Section 04a below.

5

Add Reason & Upload Document

Enter a reason for the leave. For leave types that require a supporting document (Medical Leave, Hospitalisation, etc.), upload the document before submitting. See Section 04b.

6

Submit

Click Submit Application. The status immediately becomes Pending and your Approver is notified. Days are reserved from your balance until a decision is made.

04a — Half-Day Leave

Half-day leave is only available for leave types that allow it (e.g. Annual Leave). It counts as 0.5 days against your balance.

  • Enable the Half Day toggle on the form
  • Select AM (you leave at noon) or PM (you arrive after noon)
  • Start date and end date must be the same day for a half-day application

04b — Document Upload

Certain leave types require a supporting document. The form will prompt you when a document is needed.

  • Medical Leave — upload your Medical Certificate (MC)
  • Hospitalisation Leave — upload hospital admission letter or discharge summary
  • Maternity / Paternity Leave — upload birth certificate or hospital document
  • Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG
  • You can upload additional documents after submission from the application detail page
You cannot submit an application if the dates overlap with an existing Pending or Approved application. Cancel the existing application first if needed.

Roles & Responsibilities

Two roles participate in the leave process.

leave:apply

Applicant

Check leave balances
Submit leave applications
Upload supporting documents
Cancel own Pending or Approved applications
View own application history

leave:approve

Approver

View all pending applications in the Approvals queue
Approve → Approved (with optional comment)
Reject with reason → Rejected
Cancel any application on behalf of applicant
Cannot approve their own application
07

Cancelling a Leave Application

You can cancel an application in either Pending or Approved status. The balance is restored automatically.

Cancel a Pending Application

The application has not yet been reviewed.

  1. Open the application from My Leave
  2. Click Cancel Application
  3. Confirm the cancellation
  4. Status changes to Cancelled — pending days are returned to your balance

Cancel an Approved Application

The leave has been approved but you no longer need it.

  1. Open the application from My Leave
  2. Click Cancel Application
  3. Confirm the cancellation
  4. Status changes to Cancelled — used days are returned to your balance

Notify your manager separately if cancelling approved leave affects work coverage.

Cancelled applications cannot be reopened. Submit a new application if you still need the leave.
